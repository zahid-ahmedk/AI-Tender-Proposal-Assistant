SUBJECT: Formal Pre-Tender Clarification (PTC) Batch #3 — Requests for Ambiguity Resolution under FAR 52.215-1

Dear Ms. Vance and Members of the Technical Evaluation Board,

In accordance with RFP Section 1.8 (Contractor Inquiries, Pre-Bid Interpretation, and Addenda Distribution), Apex Cloud Solutions, on behalf of its consortium bidding team, respectfully submits the following formal Schedule of Clarifications regarding Solicitation RFP-2025-0841. The items detailed below address critical specification divergence between original solicitation baseline clauses and recently distributed Addendum #03.

These queries are raised to ensure uniform cost basis, avoid unpriced risk margins during pricing proposal formulation, and preserve full compliance with federal information security mandates.

Schedule of Formal Inquiries (Batch #03)

Pre-Tender Inquiries #015 through #016
Item & Ref RFP Citation & Latent Conflict Clarification Sought & Proposed Alternative Redaction Status
PTC-015 § 4.1.2 • Add. 03 Priority: High

FIPS 140-3 Module Mandate vs. Industry Testing Queues

“Addendum #03 (Item 14, p. 8) specifies: ‘All data-at-rest hardware cryptographic modules must possess active FIPS 140-3 Level 3 validation at time of initial operational capability (IOC).’”

Conflict: Baseline RFP Section 4.1.2 permitted grandfathered FIPS 140-2 modules through CMVP transition window.

Will the Government permit a 180-day grace bridge period for modules actively listed in the NIST CMVP Review Queue?

Vendor Recommendation: Accept certified FIPS 140-2 Level 3 modules paired with active NIST CMVP FIPS 140-3 laboratory testing submission receipts (IUT / Pending List) to ensure competitive bidding and prevent single-OEM lock-in.

PTC-016 § 7.1.4 (Att. D) Priority: Medium

Cumulative SLA Penalty Cap Escalation

Attachment D (Service Level Commitments) imposes escalating liquidated damages up to 100% of quarterly cloud billings for Zone outages exceeding 120 minutes, conflicting with FAR 52.212-4(f) Excusable Delays.

Confirmation that catastrophic upstream carrier power disruption constitutes an Excusable Delay under standard federal terms.

Vendor Recommendation: Align liquidated damages ceiling to standard GSA Multiple Award Schedule caps (maximum 20% of monthly invoice value).
